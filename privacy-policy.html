Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Guests

If you do not sign in, your settings, recent results, presets, saved texts and lesson progress are kept only in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to us.

Accounts (when enabled)

If you create an account, the site uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. We store: your email address (held by Firebase Authentication), your username, display name, optional biography, social links and keyboard description, your settings, presets, saved texts and lesson progress, your test results (including per-second statistics and a recording of keystroke timing used for replays), your friends and notifications.

What is public

  • Your username, display name and join date are visible to other users.
  • Biography, social links, keyboard info, activity and best results are visible only while your profile is set to public.
  • Valid results appear on leaderboards unless you opt out of leaderboards in Account settings.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

Third-party sign-in

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, that provider tells Firebase your account identifier and email address. We do not receive your provider password.

Cookies and local storage

Local storage is used for settings and results. Firebase may store a sign-in session in your browser. The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Google Fonts are loaded from Google’s servers, which receives your IP address when fonts are requested.

Deleting your data

Use Account settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This deletes your data from our database. Backups and operational logs held by Firebase may persist for a limited period for security and legal reasons. Guests can clear local data from Settings → Danger zone or the cookie preferences dialog.

Contact

Use the Contact option in the site footer.
