Security Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

We take the security of Typing Panda and your data seriously and describe here what is actually in place.

What we do

  • Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Firestore Security Rules restrict every read and write: you can only change your own data, private details are only readable by you, and result submissions are checked for plausible values.
  • Leaderboard results are checked in your browser and again by security rules (speed limits, character counts, flags). Suspicious results are flagged instead of ranked.
  • Usernames are unique; friend requests are one document per pair, so duplicates cannot be created.
  • User-supplied text is escaped before being displayed.

Limits

Typing Panda does not claim any formal security certification. Rules cannot detect every cheating technique, so rankings are best-effort.

Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately using the Contact option in the site footer, and give us reasonable time to fix them before disclosing publicly. Do not access other users’ data while testing.
